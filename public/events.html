Design the serving moment

Tell us how your guests will enjoy their drinks: a staffed coffee station, a catered meal, a reception or a corporate gathering. Review product suitability with your beverage provider and plan how the edible cup will be introduced to guests.

Coordinate with your service team

Your catering and venue teams can assess service conditions, handling and allergen communication. Request samples and current product details before committing the cups to your event plan. Share any venue or catering requirements in your inquiry.
