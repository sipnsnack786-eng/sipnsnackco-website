Wholesale edible cups: buyer questions

Who can order edible cups in bulk?

Sip N Snack Co. welcomes inquiries from cafés, coffee shops, chai businesses, restaurants, caterers, hotels, event organizers and food-service distributors in Canada and the United States.

Can I request a sample before placing a larger order?

Yes. Business buyers can request sample information to assess suitability for their beverage service and discuss wholesale requirements.

What information should I include in a wholesale inquiry?

Share your business type, city, intended application, estimated monthly volume and preferred cup size. We can discuss current availability, pricing and shipping arrangements for your specific order.
