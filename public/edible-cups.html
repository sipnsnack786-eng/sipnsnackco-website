EDIBLE CUPS FOR BUSINESS IN TORONTO & CANADA

What is an edible cup?

An edible cup combines beverage service with an additional tasting experience. Instead of treating the cup only as packaging, your business can make it part of the serve. The exact ingredients, size, flavour, serving conditions and shelf life depend on the product selected; request current documentation before choosing a cup for your menu.

Where can your business use edible cups?

Café and chai-shop operators can evaluate cups alongside their existing drinks. Hotels and restaurants can consider guest beverage service, while professional caterers and corporate event teams can assess coffee stations and hosted functions. Distributors can review product suitability for the business customers they supply. These are applications to evaluate, rather than guarantees that every beverage or service condition will be suitable.

How should your team evaluate a sample?

Start with your actual beverage and service process. Check the product's serving specifications, handling instructions and allergen information. During a trial, assess the presentation, your team's handling workflow and the tasting experience. Record what works for your setting before planning a wholesale order. Ask us about sample availability and delivery arrangements for your location.
